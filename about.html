About us

Naman Uttarakhand is a travel and experiences business based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India. We plan and run trips across Garhwal and Kumaon — from short weekend routes to longer multi-day journeys — working directly with local hosts, drivers and guides.

What we do

  • Plan custom itineraries based on your dates, pace and budget.
  • Book vetted stays — homestays, hotels and resorts.
  • Arrange transport with experienced hill drivers.
  • Organise guided treks, river activities, temple circuits and village walks.
  • Handle permits and local formalities where they are needed.

How we work

We quote in writing, confirm in writing, and tell you plainly what is and is not included. Where a service is delivered by an independent operator, we say so. Where the mountains decide otherwise — weather, roads, closures — we tell you early and offer alternatives.
